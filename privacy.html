Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly — name, email address, telephone number, accreditation status, investment interest, and anything you write in a message — when you submit a form on this site. We also collect limited technical information automatically, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring source, through standard web analytics.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, schedule and prepare for calls, send the investor packet and investor letter if you request them, provide offering information to verified accredited investors, meet our record-keeping obligations under applicable securities regulations, and improve the website.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Who we share it with

Service providers who act on our behalf and are bound to protect it — including our email and customer relationship platform, accreditation verification provider, fund administrator, and professional advisors — and regulators or law enforcement where required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses cookies necessary for its operation and, if enabled, analytics cookies to understand aggregate usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings. [Confirm the specific analytics and advertising tools in use, and whether a consent mechanism is required for your visitors.]

Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as necessary to serve the relationship and to satisfy legal and regulatory record-keeping requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and may unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the link in any message or by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional statutory rights. [Counsel to confirm applicable state and international privacy regimes.]

Security
