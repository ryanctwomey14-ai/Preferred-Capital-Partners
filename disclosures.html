No offer of securities

Nothing on this website constitutes an offer to sell, or the solicitation of an offer to buy, any security. Any such offer will be made only to verified accredited investors through definitive offering documents, including a private placement memorandum, limited partnership or operating agreement, and subscription agreement. In the event of any inconsistency between this website and those documents, the offering documents govern in all respects.

Regulation D, Rule 506(c)

Offerings sponsored by Preferred Capital Partners are made in reliance on the exemption from registration provided by Rule 506(c) of Regulation D under the Securities Act of 1933. Participation is limited to accredited investors as defined in Rule 501(a), and accredited status must be verified by a third party. Self-certification is not sufficient. Securities offered are not registered with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission or any state securities regulator, and no regulator has passed upon the merits of, or given approval to, any offering.

Risk of loss

Investing in private real estate involves substantial risk, including the risk of losing your entire investment. Risks include, without limitation: illiquidity and the absence of any secondary market; the use of leverage, which magnifies both gains and losses; interest rate and refinancing risk; changes in local and national real estate markets; new supply; increases in operating expenses including insurance, property taxes, and payroll; execution risk in renovation and property management; environmental and casualty risk; changes in tax law; and reliance on the sponsor as manager. Each offering's private placement memorandum contains a complete description of the risks applicable to that offering and should be read in full before investing.
